Born from a small hydroponic project

Plant Habitat is a hydroponics enterprise based in San Fernando, Pampanga. Founded in 2021, it began as a small hydroponic farming project and grew into a provider of produce, systems, materials, greenhouse construction, consultations, and workshops.
